# Task Plot Brief

Title: Retrieval-Induced Forgetting
Construct: retrieval-induced forgetting / episodic retrieval inhibition

## Participant-visible session order

1. Study 48 category-exemplar pairs, 5 s per pair.
2. Retrieval-practice 12 Rp+ pairs for three rounds (36 trials), up to 10 s per cue; type the complete Chinese word and press Enter.
3. Complete 120 arithmetic parity judgments, 10 s per trial (20 min total); press F for odd and J for even.
4. Complete 48 final cued-recall trials, up to 7 s per cue; Rp- and Nrp items precede Rp+ items to reduce output interference.

## Representative participant screens

- Study: `类别：水果` above `苹果`.
- Retrieval practice: `提取练习`, `类别：颜色`, `词语线索：红＿＿`, an editable response box, and `回车` submission.
- Distractor: `7 + 4 = 11`, with `F 奇数` and `J 偶数`.
- Final test: `最终回忆`, `类别：水果`, `词语线索：苹＿＿`, an editable response box, and `回车` submission.

No feedback is shown. The item-status labels Rp+, Rp-, and Nrp are analysis labels and are not displayed on participant screens.
